About Me

I am a Software Engineer at Google on the Android LLVM Toolchain team. I help maintain and release the Android LLVM Compiler Toolchain to first-party developers and through the Android NDK. I also contribute upstream to LLVM on behalf of Android, landing fixes and features, and co-organize the LLVM Bay Area Monthly Meetup.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Dr. Santosh Pande . My research spanned the intersection of compilers, programming languages, and systems security. Before that, I was a Research Assistant at Carnegie Mellon University with Dr. Iliano Cervesato and Dr. Giselle Reis , where I also earned my B.S. in Computer Science.

Outside of work, I enjoy video games (currently Halo Campaign), TV shows (currently The Pitt), manga (One Piece), and trying new restaurants. I also love to travel — I have visited 25 countries and over 60 cities. My most recent trips took me across the United States for the FIFA World Cup 2026.
